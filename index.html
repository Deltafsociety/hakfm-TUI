The Philosophy · Zero Bloat

Simple utilities shouldn’t consume gigabytes of your memory.

Modern applications have largely abandoned lean, efficient programming. Everything today arrives packaged in a multi-hundred-megabyte Chromium engine or Electron wrapper. Opening a single streaming tab just to play ambient music while your compiler runs already eats 1.5 GB to 2 GB of RAM and leaves telemetry churn in the background.

hakfm was built to prove software can still be fast, focused, and respectful of your hardware. Written in native Rust with Ratatui and backed by headless mpv, real-world benchmarks show that hakfm consistently operates at a tested ~15MB of RAM. No Electron runtime, no analytics, no web overhead — just sound and terminal focus.
